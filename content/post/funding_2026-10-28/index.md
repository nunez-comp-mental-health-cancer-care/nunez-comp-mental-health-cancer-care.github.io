We are grateful for several new awards this year that support the lab's research:

- **Canadian Cancer Society Emerging Scholars Research Grant:** Dr. Nunez received this grant to develop and evaluate our AI-powered cancer care navigation assistant, which helps patients find supportive care services at the right time.
- **UBC Faculty of Medicine New Faculty Research Award:** supports work on evaluating the safety and reliability of AI-powered cancer care navigation systems.
- **IMH Marshall Fellowship:** Clinical Research Fellow Dr. Kal Samarasinghe received a UBC Institute of Mental Health Marshall Fellowship, supervised by Dr. Nunez, to integrate structured reasoning into our chatbot for guideline-based treatment of mood disorders.
- **Mitacs Accelerate:** Postdoctoral fellow Dr. Maryam Kamal joins the lab through a Mitacs Accelerate partnership with Sozo Intuition, developing an expert-level AI framework for real-time biotechnology intelligence.
- **Canadian Cancer Society Challenge Grant:** Dr. Nunez is a co-investigator on Co.21-IMPACT, led by Dr. Kristin Campbell, which aims to implement physical activity programs for people with colorectal cancer.

Thank you to our funders and partners for supporting this work!
