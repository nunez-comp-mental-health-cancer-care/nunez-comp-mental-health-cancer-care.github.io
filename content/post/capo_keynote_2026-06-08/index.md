Dr. Nunez delivered a keynote symposium at the [41st Annual Canadian Association of Psychosocial Oncology (CAPO) Conference](https://www.capo.ca/page-1075612), held June 8–9, 2026 at the Sheraton Hotel Newfoundland in St. John's, NL. This year's conference theme was "A New Horizon: Expanding the Access and Impact of Psychosocial Oncology."

The talk explored the potential benefits and risks of modern AI technology in psychosocial oncology, encouraging the audience to consider both the transformative opportunities and the important harms that need to be carefully managed as these tools are adopted in cancer care.
