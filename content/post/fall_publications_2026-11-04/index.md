Three papers led by the lab have recently been published or accepted:

- **A chatbot for the management of bipolar disorder** (*Canadian Journal of Psychiatry*). We built a chatbot that uses an open-weight large language model to answer clinical questions, grounded in the CANMAT and ISBD 2018 bipolar disorder guidelines. Led by former undergraduate research assistant Yash Mali, with Jiao Zeng, Kayoung Heo, Grace Zhang and Antarip Kashyap.
- **Towards clinical implementation of AI in cancer care** (*Implementation Science Communications*). A concept-mapping analysis of a province-wide workshop with patients, clinicians and leaders on what it will take to bring AI into cancer care in BC. Led by former MSc student Hong Hao Xu, with Chhavi Nayyar and Dan Hilbers.
- **Social isolation, psychological distress and survival in cancer** (*JCO Oncology Practice*, accepted). A population-based study of how social isolation and psychological distress relate to overall survival in people with cancer. Led by medical student Danlin Zeng, with Jenny Zhang and Olivia Cook.

Congratulations to everyone involved!
