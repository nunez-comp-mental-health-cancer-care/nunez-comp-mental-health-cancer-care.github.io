It has been a busy fall for sharing the lab's work.

- **ISBD 2026 (Vancouver, Sep 30):** Dr. Nunez gave an oral presentation at the International Society for Bipolar Disorders Annual Conference on our chatbot that answers clinical questions using the CANMAT/ISBD bipolar disorder guidelines. The chatbot paper is now published in the *Canadian Journal of Psychiatry*.
- **Myeloma Canada Scientific Roundtable (Montreal, Nov 7):** Dr. Nunez spoke on the opportunities and pitfalls of AI for better cancer care, drawing on lessons from our AI cancer care navigator.
- **Grand rounds:** Dr. Nunez presented on what AI actually is and how we are using it in psychiatry at Sinai Health (Oct 9) and Royal Columbian Hospital (Oct 13). He also spoke on using AI to predict who needs psychosocial support at the Canadian Geriatric Oncology Conference (Oct 2).
- **Trainee presentations:** Medical student Leah T'ien gave an oral presentation at the Canadian Academy of Consultation-Liaison Psychiatry Annual Meeting (Sep 24) on how AI should talk to patients with cancer about psychosocial needs. Medical student Danlin Zeng presented a poster at the 2026 ASCO Quality Care Symposium in Boston (Oct 16–17) on how social isolation relates to survival in cancer.

Congratulations to Leah and Danlin!
